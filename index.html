About the author

Jeremy Straub

CEO, Coastal Wealth

Jeremy has spent his career inside the wealth of other families. He has also watched it from the other side: in 2008 his father's car-dealership enterprise, worth around $200 million at its peak, was gone in a shockingly short span of time. Years later, a throat cancer diagnosis at three in the morning made one question unavoidable: what would his kids' lives look like if he weren't here?

He calls himself the bridge generation: raised by one generation, building for three more. This book is the system he built for his own family, and now for yours.

“The real challenge isn't building assets. It's building heirs.”

Questions

When does the book come out?+

Mid-2027. Founding Readers hear first and receive Chapter One before then.

Who is it for?+

Founders and family leaders, the next generation who will inherit the responsibility, and the advisors who serve them.

Is this a finance book?+

No. Money is one of seven capitals, and the book argues it is the most fragile. The rest is about people: governance, preparation, health, rituals and time.

Do I need to be wealthy?+

No. You don't need a private jet or a family office to build a 100-year family. You need the truth about why legacies collapse and a plan to start now.
