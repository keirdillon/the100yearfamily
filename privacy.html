Privacy

Your information, plainly.

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This site is operated by The 100-Year Family ("we") for the book of the same name by Jeremy Straub. This page explains what we collect when you join the Founding Readers and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Your email address and, if you give it, your first name.
  • Which sign-up form you used on this page.
  • Anonymous, aggregate visit statistics (pages viewed, referring site, device type). These do not use cookies and do not identify you.

How we use it

  • To send you Chapter One, The Family Dispatch, and news about the book and related events.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how people find the page.

We do not sell or rent your information.

Who helps us

We use a small number of service providers to run this site and send email: Vercel (hosting and privacy-friendly analytics) and Resend (email delivery and contact storage). They process your information only to provide those services to us.

Your choices

  • Every email includes an unsubscribe link. One click and you are off the list.
  • To see, correct, or delete what we hold about you, reply to any email we send you.

Questions

Reply to any email from us. A direct contact address will be posted here before the book launches.
